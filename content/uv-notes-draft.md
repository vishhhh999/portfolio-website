# UV notes: DRAFT prompts, not for the live site

> **DRAFT. Nothing here ships.** The UV lamp reveals a project's hidden "proofer's notes":
> the reasoning behind the work, written as short technical marks on the object and its photos.
> Every note that was on the site before was invented, so all of them are gone. Under UV the site
> now shows only physical fluorescence (white ink, paper) for every project, with **no notes**
> until you approve some.
>
> For each project, answer one to three of the prompts **in your own words**, as a short label
> (max ~40 characters, mono caps on screen). Use numbers only if they are real (your actual grid,
> your actual dieline). When a project's notes are ready, put them in that project's file as
> `uvNotes: [{ text: '…', anchor: [x, y, z] }]` and they appear in UV mode.
>
> The prompts lean on your own case copy (imported from the live site), not on guesses.

## Too Yumm: DRAFT
- Your copy says each flavour gets its own colour and illustration. What rule decides a flavour's colour?
- "Roasted, Not Fried" had to carry real weight. What on the pack makes that claim read first?
- What has to stay identical across every flavour for it to still read as Too Yumm on shelf?

## JSW Sports: DRAFT
- "More than a scoreboard": what does the layout do that a results page wouldn't?
- "Structure for weight": what is the one structural rule that holds every spread together?
- Where did you deliberately break that structure, and why?

## Mitooshi: DRAFT
- "Filter, not funnel": what on the page is there to turn the wrong visitor away?
- "Precision meets texture": where does the ASCII art stop being decoration and start carrying meaning?
- What did you leave out on purpose to avoid the generic crypto look?

## Sonde: DRAFT
- "The problem": in one line, why is off-brand content a demand signal rather than disobedience?
- What does the owner see first in the app, and why that first?
- What is the one interaction the whole product is built around?

## House of Hex: DRAFT
- "No home for the work": what did the old setup fail to do for the studio's portfolio?
- "Letting work lead": how does the grid stay out of the way of very different projects?
- What was the hardest piece of content to fit into the system, and how did you solve it?

## Bengal T20 League: DRAFT
- "Beyond the cliché": which obvious cricket or Bengal cliché did you refuse, and what replaced it?
- "Built from Bengal": what local source did the identity actually come from?
- What has to survive when the mark shrinks to a ticket or grows to a stadium wrap?

## SOOK: DRAFT
- "Premium, still warm": what single choice keeps the boxes from feeling cold or clinical?
- "Quiet storytelling": where does the story live on the pack: illustration, copy, or structure?
- How do the three blends differ while still reading as one family?

## SHUNYA: DRAFT
- "Built to disappear": how does the packaging express camphor sublimating?
- "Losing mass": what is the rule behind the wordmark thinning across the range?
- What is the role of the Devanagari next to the Latin wordmark?

## Indo Thai: DRAFT
- "Making scale visible": what in the hero shows the size of the operation before any copy is read?
- "Motion before copy": why does the 3D scene lead, and what does it need to say in the first seconds?
- What did the design system have to standardise for the rest of the site to be built fast?
